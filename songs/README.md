# Dedicated Offline Music Folder 🎵

This is your dedicated offline music directory!

## How to use:
1. **Drop your music files here**:
   - Copy any `.mp3`, `.wav`, `.ogg`, `.flac`, `.m4a`, or `.aac` files directly into this `songs/` folder.
   - Recommended filename format: `Artist - Title.mp3` or just `Title.mp3`.

2. **Index your songs**:
   - Option A: In the Spotify Web App, click **"Open Folder"** or **"Scan Songs"** in the top bar and pick this folder. The app reads all files directly with zero setup!
   - Option B: Drag and drop your audio files or this whole folder directly into the Spotify window.
   - Option C: Double click `scan-songs.bat` in the root folder to auto-generate `songs.json`.

3. **Enjoy pure offline playback**:
   - No internet required.
   - Real-time visualizer, equalizer, playlists, liked songs, queue, and lyrics supported.
